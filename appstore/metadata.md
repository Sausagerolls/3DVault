## Description

3DVault is the companion app for 3DVault, a free, open-source library for your 3D printing files that runs on your own server, NAS or Docker host.

Requires a 3DVault server. Setup instructions are at github.com/Sausagerolls/3DVault.

BROWSE AND FIND
• Every STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP and G-code file in one place
• Real 3D previews you can spin, zoom and view full screen
• Search by name, tag or file, and filter by category, status, rating or favourite
• Collections, including smart collections that update themselves

ORGANISE
• Categories, tags and flags such as "To print" and "Printed"
• Rate models and keep notes
• Edit many models at once
• Pin your favourite collections and tags to the sidebar

UPLOAD FROM ANYWHERE
• Upload single files, whole folders or zip archives
• Share files from other apps straight into your library (iPhone and iPad)
• Add photos of your prints from the camera or your photo library (iPhone and iPad)
• Watch a folder and upload anything saved into it (Mac)
• Duplicate files are detected and skipped

PRINT
• Open models in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer or Cura with one click (Mac)
• Send files to your Mac with AirDrop (iPhone and iPad)
• Keep models offline for when you're away from your server

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your files stay on your own server. The app has no accounts with us, no analytics and no tracking.

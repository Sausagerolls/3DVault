# 3DVault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

3DVault is a client for a 3DVault server that you run yourself, for example on a home server or NAS. This policy covers the 3DVault apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## What the apps collect

Nothing. The developer of 3DVault does not run any servers for the apps, and the apps do not contain analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code.

## Where your data goes

- **Your server.** The apps send your username, password, model files, photos, notes and tags only to the server address you enter. That server belongs to you, or to whoever runs it for you. Its operator controls that data.
- **Your device.** The apps store a sign-in token in the system keychain. They keep caches of thumbnails, previews and downloaded files, plus the models you choose to keep offline. Deleting the app removes all of this.
- **Apps you choose.** When you share a file or open it in a slicer, the app hands the file to the app or person you pick.

## Permissions

- **Local network:** used to reach your server on your home network.
- **Camera and Photos (iPhone and iPad):** used only when you take or choose a photo of a print to add to a model.
- **Files and folders (Mac):** the app reads only the files and folders you choose, and the Inbox folder if you set one.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the project repository. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Sausagerolls/3DVault/issues.
